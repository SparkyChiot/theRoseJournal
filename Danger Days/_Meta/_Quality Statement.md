### Definitions
**Canon** - any information directly mentioned in one or more canon sources.

**Canon Sources/Material** - includes the Danger Days album, official music videos, the Killjoys: California comic, or official promotional materials including but not limited to official twitter accounts and webpages.

**Implied Canon** - any information strongly implied by canon material and that is consistent with other canon sources, but is *not* directly stated.

**Fanon** - any information not referenced by canon material but used or suggested by fans to expand upon the canon. 

**Hybrid Info** - meaning an article contains both canon and fanon information that builds upon canon.

### Quality Statement
1. Canon information must be properly cited with at least one source from canon material
2. Canon information takes priority over implied canon or fanon sources.
3. Implied canon must be marked as such and cited with the source it is implied from.
4. Fanon information must be *clearly* marked as such and credit should be given to the original creator if possible.
5. Hybrid information articles must clearly outline and separate which information is canon and which is fanon
6. Each entry must follow the [[_style guide|style guide]] 
7. Each entry must do its best to use neutral tone and not present unverified info as truth.
8. If the original author of a term cannot be found or is lost, the most recent mention of the term will be listed and it must be noted that the original author could not be found
9. Uncertainty should be marked unverified and alternate possibilities should be stated.

### Citation format

### Our Manifesto
The Killjoy Archive values the preservation, collection, and spread of lore-accurate and fan-created information about the Danger Days universe created by My Chemical Romance. We understand that information about the Danger Days universe is fractured across many mediums and can be difficult to piece together, and we hope to create a space where that story becomes whole and can be filled in and built upon.

It must be noted we do not claim ownership of any information posted on this page, we are simply archivists. The Killjoy Archive is also strictly against generative AI, especially in creative spaces and thus will not use AI in any of its projects.
