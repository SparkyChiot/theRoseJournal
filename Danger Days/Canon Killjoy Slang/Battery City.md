/ˈbætᵊri ˈsɪti/ • proper noun

The city controlled by[[BLI| BL/ind]]. 

It is the only known civilization left in America, located in what was previously Los Angeles, California. BL/ind has eradicated most color, emotion, freedom, and expression in the city. It is also the home of BL/ind's headquarters.^[1]
