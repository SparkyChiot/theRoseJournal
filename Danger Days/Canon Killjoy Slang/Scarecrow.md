(**S/C/A/R/E/C/R/O/W**)
Elite agents of BL/ind who command subunits of Draculoids to exterminate threats to the industry’s dominance in the zones. Scarecrows are expected to keep a steady kill count and avoid unnecessary emotional attachments that would affect their ability to kill others.

n. Refers to BL/ind’s security department (Scarecrow Unit) or operatives of it. The operatives are of higher rank than the Draculoids and are above the police.
